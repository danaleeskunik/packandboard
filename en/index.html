Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. The app opens in a browser from a link, and you can add it to your phone’s home screen so it looks like an app.

Does it work without internet?

Some things, like maps, weather, exchange rates and translation, need an internet connection. Check with us in advance what matters to you to work offline too.

Who can see our details?

Anyone who has the link. There is no sign-up or password, so send the link only to the people traveling with you.
