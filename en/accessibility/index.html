Accessibility statement

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Pack & Board wants its website and apps to be usable by everyone. We work to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA and the Israeli standard SI 5568.

What we checked

On 31 October 2026 we ran an automated scan (axe-core) on the website pages and the sample apps, on every screen, in Hebrew and in English. We fixed what it found: color contrast, the page language in the English version, titles for maps, and labels for form fields. Every new app is checked with the same scan before it is published.

What is not checked yet

  • We have not yet done a full manual check with a screen reader and keyboard-only navigation on every screen.
  • Personal client apps are being rebuilt with the fixes step by step. Some may not include them yet.
  • The Google Maps embedded in the daily plans are third-party content, and their accessibility is Google's responsibility.

Found a problem?

If something on the website or in an app is not accessible to you, tell us what and where, and we will try to fix it soon. You can also ask for the information in another way.
