הוספת טיול

  • קישור: לוחצים על הקישור לטיול שקיבלתם מסוכן הנסיעות או מהמדריך. אם האפליקציה מותקנת, הטיול נפתח בה ונשמר.

  • קוד QR: באפליקציה לוחצים על + ואז על "סריקת קוד QR".

Support

The Pack & Board iPhone app

Adding a trip

  • Link: tap the trip link you got from your travel agent or guide. If the app is installed, the trip opens in it and is saved.
  • Paste: copy the link, then in the app tap + and "Paste the link from the clipboard".
  • QR code: in the app, tap + and "Scan a QR code".

Back to your trips

Inside a trip, tap the round suitcase button in the bottom corner.

Removing a trip

In the trip list, press and hold the trip and confirm.

Notifications from your guide

  • You get notifications only from the trips you saved, when the guide sends a message.
  • Not getting them? Check Settings → Apps → Pack & Board → Notifications, and that no Focus mode is blocking them.

Without internet

A trip you opened once in the app is kept on the phone and opens offline too.
