Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · עברית

Pack & Board builds personal digital trip guides, on the web and in the Pack & Board iPhone app. This page explains what information we use, why, where it is kept, and how to ask us to delete it.

The Pack & Board iPhone app

The app keeps your trips and opens them. It has no sign-up, no password, no ads and no tracking, and it does not collect your name, phone number or location.

WhatWhere and why
The trip links you saved, and a short summary of each trip (name, dates, destination)On your phone only. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you delete the app.
The cameraOnly when you tap "Scan a QR code", to read the trip link. The image is not saved or sent.
Notifications from your guide: the phone's technical push address, the trip's id, the app's language and a random chat idOnly if you allowed notifications on the phone. Kept in Supabase, so that we can send you your guide's messages about that trip only. The notification itself goes through Apple's push service. When you turn notifications off or delete the app, the address stops working and is removed from the list.
Messages to the group chat and to the guide: the text you wrote and the name you chose to showKept in Supabase, so that the guide and the group can see them. Only people who have the trip's link can see them.

What we collect on the website

WhatWhere and why
Visits to the website and the example guides: which pages were opened, device type, approximate country and city, and where you came fromGoogle Analytics, to see whether the site works. No name, phone number or email address. Ad personalisation is off. The personal trip guides have no analytics.
Trip details you send us: destination, dates, flights, hotels, bookings and plansOnly to build your personal guide. Please don't send card numbers, passports or passwords. They are not needed.
Files you upload on the upload page: documents, emails, screenshots and text about the tripKept privately on Cloudflare, and only we can see them. Used to build your guide. We may send them to an AI tool provider (Anthropic) to read them. You can delete any file yourself and ask for full deletion. Files are deleted after the trip.
Clicks on partner links on the website's home pages: that you came from us to a booking site (for example Booking or GetYourGuide)Travelpayouts, so that we earn a commission if you book. Your price does not change. No name, phone number or trip details are sent. This script is not in the personal guides or in the app.
Messages to us on WhatsApp or by emailTo answer you and arrange the work.

Where the information is kept

  • Supabase: the guide's messages and the chat, and the list of phones that allowed notifications in the app.
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service): delivers the notifications to the iPhone.
  • Google (Google Analytics): website visit statistics.
  • Cloudflare: web notification sign-ups, the latest update for each trip, and the files uploaded on the upload page.
  • Travelpayouts: the travel-service links on the website's home pages.
  • GitHub Pages: the website and the guides are hosted there. Personal guides are encrypted and open only with their link.
  • These services work mostly outside Israel, so information may be transferred to and kept on servers abroad.

Who can see your guide

A personal guide opens with its link, without a password. Anyone who has the link can see what is in it. Share it only with the people you travel with.

Notifications

  • Notifications go only to people who signed up for that specific trip.
  • You can turn them off at any time: in the iPhone app, in Settings → Apps → Pack & Board → Notifications; on the web, with the bell button in the guide.
  • Notification text may show on the lock screen, so we never put booking numbers, door codes or personal details in it.

How long we keep it

We keep trip details only while the guide is active or until you ask us to delete them. Google Analytics statistics are kept according to its settings.

Deletion and requests

You can ask at any time to see what information we have about you, to correct it or to delete it: your guide, your trip details, your messages and your notification sign-up. Write to us and we will handle it promptly. The trips saved in the app are deleted from the phone by pressing and holding a trip, or by deleting the app.

Children

Pack & Board is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information about them.
